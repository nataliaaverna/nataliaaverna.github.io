Natalia Averna.

PhD student in Mathematical Research

I am a PhD student in the Mathematical Research programme at Universidad Complutense de Madrid. My thesis focuses on topological dynamics, supervised by Jaime Jorge Sánchez Gabites and Héctor Barge Yáñez.

I am a member of the Department of Algebra, Geometry and Topology at the Faculty of Mathematical Sciences, where I hold a UCM predoctoral research contract.

I hold two bachelor’s degrees, one in Mathematics and one in Physics, completed through a double-degree programme, as well as a master’s degree in Advanced Mathematics, all from Universidad Complutense de Madrid.
